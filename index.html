Dr. Awadhesh Sharma currently works as a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Civil Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IIT Roorkee)[cite: 1]. He earned his Ph.D. in Civil Engineering from IIT Roorkee, specializing in mechanical and fracture behavior of Ultra-High-Performance Fibre-Reinforced Concrete (UHPFRC)[cite: 1].

His research integrates multi-channel Acoustic Emission (AE) and full-field Digital Image Correlation (DIC) with advanced non-linear computational mechanics (Abaqus Concrete Damaged Plasticity and COMSOL Multiphysics)[cite: 1, 2]. His ongoing investigations address constitutive modeling, fatigue degradation envelopes, and the formulation of code-level design parameters for bridge and infrastructure applications under national sponsored initiatives[cite: 1, 2].

Laboratory & Testing Facilities

Fracture & 3PBT Testing

Evaluation of crack mouth opening displacement (CMOD), fracture energy, and crack-bridging behavior in UHPFRC beams[cite: 1, 2].

Digital Image Correlation (DIC)

Full-field surface strain computation, microcrack initiation tracking, and fracture process zone (FPZ) evolution[cite: 1, 2].

Acoustic Emission (AE) Monitoring

Real-time transient wave detection to characterize microcracking, fiber debonding, and tensile vs. shear crack modes[cite: 1, 2].

Mix Design Optimization

Modified Andreasen particle packing utilizing indigenous silica fume, quartz powder, and high-strength steel fibers[cite: 2].
