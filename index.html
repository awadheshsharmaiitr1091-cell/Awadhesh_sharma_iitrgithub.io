About & Executive Summary

Ph.D. scholar and Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Civil Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IIT Roorkee)[cite: 1].

My research focuses on Ultra-High-Performance Fibre-Reinforced Concrete (UHPFRC), fracture mechanics, fatigue behavior, and structural life-extension[cite: 1]. I specialize in integrating advanced experimental measurement systems—including Digital Image Correlation (DIC) and Acoustic Emission (AE) monitoring—with non-linear computational mechanics (Abaqus CDP modeling and COMSOL Multiphysics) to solve structural engineering challenges for railway and highway infrastructure[cite: 1, 2].

Experimental Diagnostics & Research Gallery

Experimental testing setups, sensor deployments, and advanced characterization methodologies utilized across my research publications[cite: 1, 2]:

Fracture & 3PBT Testing Evaluation of crack mouth opening displacement (CMOD), fracture energy (GF), and crack-bridging behavior in UHPFRC beams under monotonic and cyclic fatigue loads[cite: 1, 2].
Digital Image Correlation (DIC) Full-field surface strain computation, microcrack initiation tracking, and fracture process zone (FPZ) evolution on fiber-reinforced cementitious composites[cite: 1, 2].
Acoustic Emission (AE) Diagnostics Real-time transient elastic wave acquisition to identify fiber debonding, matrix microcracking, and shear versus tensile crack classification during mechanical loading[cite: 1, 2].
Mix Optimization & Raw Materials Modified Andreasen & Andersen (MAA) packing optimization using indigenous silica fume, quartz powder, and high-strength steel fibers mixed in high-energy planetary mixers[cite: 2].
