Dr. Awadhesh Sharma

"श्रमं विना न किमपि साध्यम्" (Without hard work, nothing can be achieved)

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Civil Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IIT Roorkee). I completed my Ph.D. in Civil Engineering at IIT Roorkee specializing in the mechanical and fracture characterization of Ultra-High-Performance Fibre-Reinforced Concrete (UHPFRC).

My primary research involves investigating fracture behavior, fatigue resistance, and size effect phenomena in cementitious composites. I integrate multi-channel Acoustic Emission (AE) and full-field Digital Image Correlation (DIC) with computational modeling in Abaqus (Concrete Damaged Plasticity) and COMSOL Multiphysics to formulate code-level design parameters for bridge and infrastructure applications.

Primary Research Interests

Sponsored Research & Industrial Projects

Investigation of constitutive tensile, compressive, and shear properties for UHPC (130–160 MPa) utilizing indigenous raw materials. Establishing codal guidelines for inclusion in IRS Concrete Bridge Codes, full-scale girder testing, and AE sensor instrumentation.

Sponsor: NBCC (India) Limited | Dec 2024 – July 2025
Executed particle packing mix optimization, mechanical characterization, and structural testing for high-performance concrete columns and building elements.

Laboratory Testing & Experimental Facilities

Experimental testing systems, non-destructive sensors, and characterization setups used across my research work:

Fracture & 3PBT Testing Evaluation of crack mouth opening displacement (CMOD) and fracture energy under monotonic and cyclic fatigue loading.
Digital Image Correlation (DIC) Optical measurement for full-field surface strain and fracture process zone (FPZ) tracking.
Acoustic Emission (AE) Monitoring Real-time transient wave detection to characterize microcracking, macrocracking, and fiber pullout stages.
Mix Optimization & Raw Materials Modified Andreasen particle packing utilizing indigenous silica fume, quartz powder, and steel fibers.
