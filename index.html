About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Civil Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IIT Roorkee). My research specializes in advanced cementitious composites, particularly Ultra-High-Performance Fiber-Reinforced Concrete (UHPFRC), fracture mechanics, fatigue behavior, and full-field non-destructive damage diagnostics.

Research Domains & Core Expertise

Ultra-High-Performance Concrete (UHPC/UHPFRC) Fracture Mechanics & Size Effect Fatigue Characterization Digital Image Correlation (DIC) Acoustic Emission (AE) Monitoring Abaqus FEA (CDP Modeling) Particle Packing Density Optimization
