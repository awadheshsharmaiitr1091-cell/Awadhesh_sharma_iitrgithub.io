About & Executive Summary

Ph.D. scholar and Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Civil Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IIT Roorkee).

My research focuses on Ultra-High-Performance Fibre-Reinforced Concrete (UHPFRC), fracture mechanics, fatigue behavior, and structural life-extension. I specialize in integrating advanced experimental measurement systems—including Digital Image Correlation (DIC) and Acoustic Emission (AE) monitoring—with non-linear computational mechanics (Abaqus CDP modeling and COMSOL Multiphysics) to solve structural engineering challenges for railway and highway infrastructure.
